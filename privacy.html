Privacy Policy

Zoro Shorts Uploader · Last updated: 21 November 2026

Zoro Shorts Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Zoro YouTube channel to upload their own videos. This page explains what data the app uses.

Google and YouTube data the app uses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app asks for permission to manage their YouTube account (scope: youtube.force-ssl). The app uses this permission only to:

  • upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own channel,
  • set each video's title, description, tags, language, audience and privacy settings,
  • read a video's status (for example, whether processing is finished),
  • set a video's thumbnail.

Where data is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. Video files and video details stay on that computer and on YouTube. The app has no server, no database and no analytics.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube, and only to carry out the uploads listed above.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's token file from the owner's computer also removes the app's access on that computer.

Children

The channel publishes content for children, but the app itself is used only by the adult channel owner. The app does not collect any information from viewers or children.
