Zoro Shorts Uploader

A private tool for one YouTube channel.

Zoro Shorts Uploader is a small personal tool used by the owner of the Zoro kids superhero animation channel. It runs only on the owner's own computer and helps them upload their own original YouTube Shorts.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, always as private first.
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags, language and audience settings.
  • Changes a video from private to public only when the owner approves it.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access anyone else's account.
